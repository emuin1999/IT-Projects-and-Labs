# Creating and Documenting a Support Ticket in Spiceworks
Objective: Practice using a ticketing system to document and track a user account lockout issue.

What I did:
- Created a support ticket in Spiceworks for a user who was unable to sign in due to multiple failed login attempts.
- Documented the user's reported issue.
- Recorded the troubleshooting steps taken to investigate the account lockout.
- Used the ticket to track the issue and document the resolution.

## What I Learned:

I learned how to handle a help desk ticket from the moment a user reports an issue until the ticket is resolved and closed. The scenario involved a user who could not log in because their Active Directory account was locked after multiple failed login attempts.

I practiced reviewing the user's ticket, examining the attached screenshot, and accepting the ticket as a help desk technician. I then used Active Directory Users and Computers (ADUC) to locate the user's account, unlock it, and reset their password.

I also learned the importance of communicating with users throughout the troubleshooting process. After resolving the issue, I notified the user, asked them to confirm that they could log in successfully, and verified that they had updated their password. Finally, I documented the troubleshooting steps and closed the ticket.

This exercise helped me understand how help desk technicians troubleshoot account access issues, communicate with users, document their work, and follow a ticket through its entire lifecycle.
